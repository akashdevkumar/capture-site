Privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026

Capture keeps what you capture on your Mac. It has no account, no analytics and no ads, and it sends nothing to us.

What Capture stores, and where

  • Your captures. Notes, screenshots, screen recordings, the text read from screenshots and recording transcripts are saved in ~/Documents/Capture. If you pick a folder for screenshots, they are saved there instead.
  • Recordings you share to Claude. A copy of the video, six still frames and the transcript are saved in ~/Documents/Capture/shared.
  • Settings. The screenshot folder you picked, your recent folders and whether Capture opens at login are kept in Capture’s preferences on your Mac.

You can open, move or delete any of these files in Finder at any time. Deleting the app does not delete your Documents folder.

Permissions, and when they are used

  • Screen Recording: only when you drag over an area or start a recording.
  • Microphone: only while the mic button is on or a recording is running.
  • Speech Recognition: to turn your voice into text with Apple’s speech engine, which runs on your Mac.

Text in screenshots is read on your Mac with Apple’s Vision framework. Capture also uses macOS to open at login, which you can turn off from its menu bar icon.

What leaves your Mac

Capture itself sends nothing over the internet. It has no servers, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising, and it does not write what you capture into the system log.

Sharing to Claude happens only when you ask. When you click Claude on a recording and pick where it goes, Capture copies a message to your clipboard and opens the Claude app. Anything you then send in Claude is handled by Anthropic under Anthropic’s privacy policy.

To list your recent Claude Code sessions, Capture reads each session’s title, project folder and last-used time from the Claude app’s files on your Mac. It does not read your conversations, and it sends none of this anywhere.

The speech and text features are part of macOS, which Apple’s privacy policy covers.

Children

Capture is not directed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version of Capture sends anything off your Mac, this page will say so before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub. Capture is made by Akash Gidde.
